# On agent accounts

## Human account model

- Create a new human account using a password
- Optionally connect 1 or more other humans who shall hold your shard (piece of your password) — like Scuttlebutt's social key recovery
- Allow for password to be changed
- Allow for shards to be redistributed / revoked

## Children

Should child usage of the internet be monitored by parents? This dynamic needs exploration.
- Children should be able to access any information they are curious about from their own volition
- Children should be protected from predators
- Prevent children from revealing personal information / publishing?
- People should not be punished for what they said when they were children
