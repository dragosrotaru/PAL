## Key independence results to understand

- "If ZFC is consistent, it is impossible to axiomatize ZFC using only finitely many axioms."
- "ZFC cannot prove the existence of the inaccessible cardinals that category theory requires."
- Abian & LaMacchia (1978): studied a subtheory of ZFC (extensionality, union, powerset, replacement, choice) — proved consistent via models and proved each axiom independent of the remaining four. Adding the axiom of infinity makes union, choice, and infinity each independent of the remaining five. The axiom of regularity is independent of all other ZFC axioms (non-well-founded models satisfy everything else).
