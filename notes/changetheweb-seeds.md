# changetheweb seeds

Unfinished thoughts and ideas from 2019–2020.

## Data model seeds

- How can you couple human assertions of truth with their own verification code?
- When a client requests an Object, it can check against the information size included in the identifier to cancel the request early
- Checking if IDs refer to real things — think about how Redis handles keys
- LEB128 or alternative for variable-length encoding?
- Infinite sequences or sequences of indeterminate length (streams)
- Breaking up long sequences into multiple Nodes/Edges?
- Timestamps, agents, signing nodes/edges?
- Node information self-describing encodings — multiformats?
- Indexing content
- Computations as objects, verification of computation
- From the existing world of DLTs: ETH, Holochain, Scuttlebutt

## UI / interface seeds

- What would procedurally generated UI look like in the HyperGraph?
- The interface of meaning between man and machine as the signature of a function — or more generally as the naming of information
- An ego check button in social media
- When thinking about HCI: the configurations are Human–Human, Human–Machine–Human, Machine–Machine, Human–Machine, Human–Human–Human, etc.
- There is a spectrum between natural language and formal language. What would a bridge look like?
- You can throw a frisbee with your eyes closed, but you must catch with your eyes open. Is it ok to allow individuals to broadcast to millions of people with their eyes closed? How can we make people see the repercussions of their actions on the web?

## Naming seeds

- Ownership over meaning (owned names) as descendants of a privileged merkle tree root. We have to challenge the idea that names have to be global. Is Search and Traverse not sufficient? SEO becomes Memetics.
- Capitalize resolvable Names within written language — like in German or TiddlyWiki to denote ProperNouns. Capitals signal importance and could mark resolvable concepts.

## Social / governance seeds

- Browser extension useful in the transition to the new web — like MetaMask for Ethereum. Highlight particular text and reference it as a source; snapshot a webpage (like Internet Archive); provide context to the old web.
- Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (explored AragonDAO as part of HackMoney Hackathon — [$HIP](https://github.com/carboclan/hip))
- Do we need organizations for human beings to work together harmoniously? Could we build an economic system predicated on total individual sovereignty?
- To defeat filter bubbles and echo chambers: get all the outside-the-box thinkers, put them inside a box and shake the box violently.
- Should a process have write access to its own code? What are the ethics of programs terminating?
- I'm thinking about making my personal wiki/todos/calendar public, minus info about friends and family. Interesting to explore the boundary between privacy and transparency.
